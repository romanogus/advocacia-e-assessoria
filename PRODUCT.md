# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual clients and families across Brazil in need of legal counsel and dispute resolution, particularly workers facing termination or labor disputes, citizens navigating social security / INSS retirement and disability benefits, and individuals dealing with civil contracts, consumer rights, family, or succession matters.

## Product Purpose

Provide an accessible, transparent, and humanized digital legal consultancy for Santos & Trevizan — Advocacia e Assessoria. The website communicates the firm's practice areas, establishes professional credibility, and enables potential clients anywhere in Brazil to obtain immediate legal guidance and consultation through direct WhatsApp channels, video calls, and email.

## Positioning

A modern, 100% digital Brazilian law practice combining technical rigor and ethical transparency with accessible, empathetic client communication. Unlike traditional, opaque law firms that require in-person visits and use intimidating legal jargon, Santos & Trevizan delivers responsive, nationwide legal assistance with clear explanations and rapid triage via WhatsApp.

## Operating Context

- **Client Interaction:** Prospective clients typically arrive with acute, stressful legal questions (job loss, denied pension/disability claim, civil dispute).
- **Communication Channels:** Primary intake and consultation occur via WhatsApp (structured pre-filled messages per practice area), video calls, and email.
- **Geographic Reach:** Nationwide (100% digital representation across Brazilian courts and administrative agencies such as INSS and TRTs).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Practice Areas:**
  - **Direito Trabalhista:** Rescisão contratual, horas extras, adicionais (insalubridade, periculosidade), FGTS, verbas rescisórias.
  - **Direito Previdenciário:** Aposentadorias, pensão por morte, benefícios por incapacidade (auxílio-doença, BPC/LOAS).
  - **Direito Civil & Família:** Análise e elaboração de contratos, direito do consumidor, divórcio, partilha de bens, inventários judiciais e extrajudiciais.
- **Technical Stack:** Astro 6, Tailwind CSS v4, Sanity CMS (content management for blog posts and practice areas), Svelte / React components.
- **Constraints:** Compliance with Brazilian Bar Association (OAB) ethical rules regarding legal advertising (informative, educational, non-mercantile communication).

## Product Principles

1. **Immediate Clarity over Bureaucracy:** Guide stressed visitors directly to clarity about their legal situation without overwhelming them with dense legalese.
2. **Humanized & Ethical Approach:** Treat every inquiry with empathy and strict ethical rigor in compliance with OAB standards.
3. **Frictionless Action:** Minimize barriers between discovering a relevant legal topic and initiating a consultation via WhatsApp.
4. **National Accessibility:** Maintain a lightweight, fast, and responsive digital experience accessible on any device across Brazil.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ensure high color contrast, clear typographic hierarchy, semantic HTML headings, screen-reader friendly content labels, and accessible touch targets for mobile users who depend primarily on smartphones for legal inquiries.
